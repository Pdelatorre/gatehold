Gatehold

Privacy Policy · Effective October 24, 2026

Gatehold is a game for iPhone published by Phillip Delatorre. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. The short version: Gatehold does not collect any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Gatehold has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

Game progress

Your gems, camp upgrades, unlocked heroes, best waves and sound settings are saved only on your iPhone. They never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The optional Supporter Pack is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The game only asks Apple whether the Supporter Pack has been bought on your Apple ID so it can unlock it.

Children

Gatehold does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Internet use

The game runs entirely on your device and works offline. The only time it connects to the internet is when Apple's App Store checks the Supporter Pack price, a purchase or a restore.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
