Support

The orc horde marches on your keep. Build towers, raise walls and push one wave further every run. Gatehold is a free game for iPhone with no ads.

Need help?

Email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com and include your iPhone model and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I play? Drag anywhere on the screen to move. Your hero attacks on their own. Spend coins on the glowing pads to recruit archers, build towers and upgrade. The camp screen has a full "How to play" guide.

I bought the Supporter Pack on another device or reinstalled the game. Open the camp screen and tap Restore purchase under the Supporter Pack.

There's no sound. Turn up your iPhone's volume and check that the Music and Sound effects switches on the camp screen are on. The speaker button during a run mutes everything.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress. A Supporter Pack purchase can always be restored.
